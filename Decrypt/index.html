DECRYPT is a movement of technology enthusiasts, creatives, learners, and builders who believe exposure to the right knowledge, people, and ideas can change the trajectory of a person's future.

The annual conference is the movement's flagship gathering. But the movement extends far beyond the conference.

Technology is changing quickly. Young people should not simply consume technology. They should understand it. Question it. Secure it. And eventually shape what comes next.

DECRYPT exists to create environments where people can encounter ideas, knowledge, people, and opportunities that push them forward.
